Schritt 2 — Kurs importieren

Lade die Kursdatei *-md.zip hoch, die du exportiert hast. Trinket embed links are automatically rewritten to point to this server.

If any linked trinkets haven't been imported yet you'll see a warning and can choose to continue anyway (old trinket.io URLs will be left intact until those trinkets are imported later).
